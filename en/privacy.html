Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller is Pipe Hero Studio. Contact person: the founder of Pipe Hero Studio. For any privacy question, please email ….

What this page collects

This page doesn't use cookies or any analytics or tracking tools.

It only collects data if you fill in and send the contact form: your name, clinic, email and/or phone number, town or city, booking system and the message you write. This data is sent to Netlify, the service hosting the page, which stores it and forwards it to us by email.

If you choose a language at the top of the page, that choice is saved only in your browser, so your next visit opens in the same language. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Third-party services

  • Fonts: the page loads its fonts from Google Fonts. To do this, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Hosting and the form: the page is hosted by Netlify (Netlify, Inc., USA), which stores the requests sent through the form and may log technical data, such as your IP address and the time of the visit, for security reasons. Transfers to the USA are covered by standard contractual clauses (and the UK addendum).
  • WhatsApp: messages you send by WhatsApp are also processed by WhatsApp (Meta), under that service's own privacy policy.

What we use your data for

If you get in touch through the form, WhatsApp or email, we receive whatever you choose to send: usually your name, clinic, email or phone number, town or city, booking system and message. We use it only to reply to you and, if we agree to, to arrange the first call and the pilot.

The lawful basis is your request to be contacted and the steps taken before any possible agreement (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR). We don't sell this data or share it for marketing. We keep it only for as long as it's needed for that purpose.

How to ask us to delete your data

Email … with the subject "Delete my data", or ask in the same WhatsApp chat. We'll delete it from Netlify and from our inbox and confirm within one month at the latest.

You can also ask to access, correct, restrict or port your data, or object to it being used, through the same contact.

Complaints

If you think your data hasn't been handled properly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk) or the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. If the page ever starts using analytics, that will only happen with your consent first.
