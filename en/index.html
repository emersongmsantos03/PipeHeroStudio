What it does

Three jobs that usually get left until the end of the day.

1

Answers

Ask the way you'd ask your receptionist: who didn't turn up yesterday, what's free on Friday, how many laser sessions were booked this month. The answer comes from your own system.

2

Sums up

Every morning, a short summary: today's diary, no-shows, gaps to fill and who should be contacted.

3

Prepares

Finds the clients who are due to come back and writes a message for each one. Your team checks it and sends it.

Follow-ups

Everyone due to come back, in one list.

The assistant compares each client's last visit with the follow-up interval for their treatment, which you set during set-up. For each client, a message is ready to check.

Morning summary

The whole day in one message, before you open the doors.

It arrives at the time you choose. In a few lines you know what needs attention today and what can wait.

How it works

From first call to everyday use.

  1. First call

    30 minutes to understand how your clinic runs, which treatments need follow-ups and what you want to know each morning.

  2. Connecting your system

    It connects to your booking system with an access key that your clinic creates and can switch off at any time.

  3. Set-up

    Follow-up intervals for each treatment, the tone of the messages, the time of the summary and who on the team receives it.

  4. Trial with your team

    Your team uses the assistant day to day and we adjust whatever needs adjusting.

Up and running in about 2 weeks.

Privacy and GDPR

Your clients' data stays your clinic's data.

Data processing agreement

Signed before anything is connected, as Article 28 of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR requires. It sets out which data is used, what for and for how long.

Access key you can revoke

Your clinic creates the key in its booking system and can switch it off whenever it likes. Access ends from that moment.

No clinical notes or photos

The assistant works with booking data: names, contact details, treatments and dates. It doesn't access clinical notes or photos.

It says it's an AI

The assistant always introduces itself as artificial intelligence and never pretends to be a member of your team.
